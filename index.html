Before you install

Your informed consent matters.

Pairing may grant Android Device Owner privileges. These can restrict app removal and some device settings. If the app requests camera, microphone, or location permission, ask the shop why it is needed before granting it.

Continue only if you understand and agree to the shop’s terms. Ask about data use, permissions, and the Release process before pairing. If Release is not completed after your final payment, contact the shop.

!
About version 1.8

This is an older app version.

Version 1.8 may include older app-control restrictions that affect installing or uninstalling Play Store apps. Version 1.9 fixed that issue. Download 1.8 only if you specifically need it; choose the updated version for normal use.

Quick answers

Good to know
before you begin.

Ask the shop to explain anything you are unsure about before installing or pairing.

Is this the app for the customer’s phone?

Yes. This is the PhoneLock Customer app. Pairing is completed separately from the shop’s Admin or Setup app.

What changes when the phone is paired?

Android Device Owner privileges may restrict app removal and some device settings. Understand the shop agreement and permissions before continuing.

What should happen after the final payment?

The shop should complete Release and confirm the PhoneLock controls are removed. Then the app can be uninstalled.

When should I use version 1.8?

Only when you specifically need this older build. The Play Store app uninstall issue was fixed in 1.9; ask the shop before using 1.8.
